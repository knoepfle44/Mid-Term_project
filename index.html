Where is the Smart City?

Distribution of digital infrastructure in Vienna's public space

Legend

The Promised City

Vienna presents itself as a smart city, but whether digital infrastructure such as public Wi-Fi, bike sharing and charging stations is equally accessible across the city remains an open question. Access to this infrastructure shapes who can move, connect and participate in urban life, which makes it central to sustainable urban development. This story map follows three kinds of infrastructure across Vienna's 23 districts to ask who the smart city is actually built for. Scroll down to explore where these services are concentrated, and where they are missing.
